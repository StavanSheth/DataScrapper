Deterministic 5-Step Verification Process for Maps, Websites & LinkedIn Org Intelligence

Tab 1: Google Maps Harvester
Master Area Pinning & Place Capture
Step 1: Geolocation Lock

Calculates lat/lon coordinates and establishes the 4km radius search bounding box on Google Maps.

Step 2: Infinite Feed Panning

Continuously scrolls the results feed and detects end banner to harvest all businesses in the area.

Step 3: Side-Panel Verification

Clicks into each place card, validates business name, category, rating, reviews, address, phone, and opening hours.

Step 4: Real-Time Map Plotting

Parses exact GPS coordinates, commits record to SQLite DB, and plots interactive pin on Leaflet map.

Step 5: Multi-Tab Queue Dispatch

Automatically hands over verified website to Tab 2 and business name to Tab 3 for parallel execution.

Tab 2: Shop Website Inspector
Anti-Bot, Consent & Email Capture
Step 1: Domain Navigation & Health

Opens official URL with anti-hang timeouts and verifies page is reachable without breaking the pipeline.

Step 2: Anti-Bot & Cloudflare Check

Detects Cloudflare Turnstile, challenge pages, Datadome, PerimeterX, and auto-handles checkboxes.

Step 3: Cookie Banner Auto-Accept

Detects OneTrust, Cookiebot, and GDPR overlays, clicks 'Accept' buttons, and logs consent status.

Step 4: Visual Screenshot Archiving

Captures landing page image (with anti-bot warning if active) and saves to persistent `/static/screenshots/`.

Step 5: Multi-Method Contact Extraction

Extracts clean contact emails, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X profiles, and passes to Tab 3.

Tab 3: LinkedIn Org Intelligence
Company Hierarchy & Leadership Mapping
Step 1: Canonical Company Resolution

Cross-references verified company page from website or Google canonical index, avoiding promotional ads.

Step 2: People Directory Access

Navigates directly to the `/people/` directory in your active Chrome profile to preserve session cookies.

Step 3: Progressive Infinite Scrolling

Smoothly triggers lazy loading to discover up to 40+ employees, executive directors, and department heads.

Step 4: Card Sanitization & Validation

Cleans name strings, strips connection degree tags (`• 1st`, `• 2nd`, `• 3rd+`), extracts titles, avatars, and `/in/` profile URLs.

Step 5: 3-Tier Seniority Classification

Classifies into 👑 Executive, 💼 Management, and 🛠️ Staff tiers, saving to SQLite and rendering the Org Tree.
